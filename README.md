# UniGuide - Universal Orlando Family Navigator

Permanent live PWA for Drake's family trip (9yo birthday, Sat/Sun mornings at Universal Orlando).

**Features**
- Complete 2-day game plan with morning priorities, birthday tips, weather-adaptive strategy, HHN notes
- Ride wait estimates (refresh for updates from public sources)
- Best food, prices, cheapest cooler strategy with specific locations
- Live weather from Open-Meteo with park impact alerts
- All tips synthesized from deep research (themeparks.wiki, TouringPlans, official policies, family reviews)

Open on your phone and tap Share → "Add to Home Screen" for the app.

Built and published autonomously.
