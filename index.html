Plan

Saturday - Universal Studios Florida: Arrive at opening for rope drop in Diagon Alley (lowest crowds in mornings per historical data). Prioritize Gringotts (40" Kid-Friendly). Mobile order lunch at Leaky Cauldron for birthday treat. Afternoon for Minions and indoor shows if rain (common PM thunderstorms in October). Cooler for snacks to save time and money. HHN likely running - focus daytime to avoid scary evening event for 9yo.

Sunday - Islands of Adventure: Rope drop Hogsmeade (Hippogriff 36" Kid-Friendly, Forbidden Journey 40" Kid-Friendly). Lunch at Mythos (top rated, kid-friendly, birthday special). Afternoon for VelociCoaster if ready or indoor alternatives if weather closes outdoors. Long stays allow flexibility for crowds and weather.

This order minimizes wait time, maximizes ride time for the family of 5, accounts for typical October crowds and weather, and avoids HHN if not included or too scary for a 9yo. All major rides assumed open; check app for real-time status.

Tips

• Download the official Universal app for live waits, mobile order, interactive map with prices and walking times.
• Small cooler with snacks and drinks permitted (no glass, no alcohol) - biggest money saver for family of 5.
• Get birthday button at Guest Services. Many restaurants will add a free cupcake or singing if you mention it at ordering.
• Mornings have the lowest waits (30-50% shorter per historical data from TouringPlans). Use single rider lines for popular rides if available.
• October HHN increases evening crowds and adds scary elements - focus on daytime if your tickets don't include HHN or if it's too intense for a 9yo.
• Weather adaptive: PM thunderstorms common. Prioritize indoor rides (Gringotts, Mummy, shows) if rain starts. Pack ponchos (cheap in park).
• The realistic family portrait on the coaster background is your crew riding with you.
